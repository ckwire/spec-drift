What does it do?

Given a spec section (text) and the corresponding drawing sheet or drawing notes (text/annotations) for the same scope of work, identify where they disagree — material mismatch, rating mismatch, dimension mismatch, or a requirement present in one but absent from the other — and cite the specific source location for each flag.

What does "correct" mean?

A flag is correct if: (1) the cited spec passage and drawing note both actually exist as cited, and (2) a reasonable person reading both would agree they conflict or that one is silent where the other isn't.

What does failure look like?

References items that are not in the drawings and not related to project or location requirements of code.
False Positives - need to expand upon this.
False Negatives - need to expand upon this.
Wrong pairing - References items not on the drawings or from differing projects or unrelated work.

Reference Items will include one real spec section and one real drawing for the same scope.
